Frequently asked

Everything you need to know

What stage should I be at to apply?

From a validated idea to an early-stage startup with first customers. What matters is ambition, the team and the market potential.

How long is the program?

12 intensive weeks, in person and remote, followed by 6 months of post-program support.

Does the incubator take equity?

Terms depend on the track you choose. We discuss it transparently during the selection interview — no surprises.

Will I meet investors?

Yes. You get access to our investor and business-angel network, and you pitch at the end-of-program Demo Day.

Do I need a team already?

No. Solo founders and formed teams can both apply. We also help you structure and complete your team.

How does the application work?

You fill in the form, we review your application, then we set up an interview. The answer comes fast — no endless process.

Ready to accelerate your startup with Leapollo?

Apply to the next cohort. Spots are limited — we select the projects where our support truly moves the needle.
